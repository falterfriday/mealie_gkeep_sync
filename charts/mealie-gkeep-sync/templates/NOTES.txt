  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} exec deploy/{{ include "mealie-gkeep-sync.fullname" . }} -- \

A red readiness with the pod still Running is expected behaviour for bad
credentials: restarting cannot fix them, so the pod stays up and inspectable.
